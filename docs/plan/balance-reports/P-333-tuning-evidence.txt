3. Rate envelope base limits (rateEnvelope.test.ts derives them; config.ts ships them)
   drops 110 -> 115, falls 300, fallValue 477 -> 1 252, fusions 127, crates 24 -> 28.
   - The burst model now runs once per cadence style's fusionShare and keeps the highest
     p99.9 × 1.25. Whole-chute play fuses least and drops the most separate coins, so it
     keeps falls at 300 (aimed alone would give 164 and flag casual players). drops and
     crates move only by sampling across four style cohorts.
   - fallValue also takes the physics ladder's busiest 30 s for every style ≤ 3 taps/s:
     3/s one spot peaks at 1 001 (rapidPinpoint 643), so 1 001 × 1.25 = 1 252. The old 517
     and 477 flagged honest 2-3 taps/s play.
   - fallValue fusionWindow elasticity 0.014 -> 0.162 = 0.773 × 0.25 / (1 + 0.773 × 0.25).
